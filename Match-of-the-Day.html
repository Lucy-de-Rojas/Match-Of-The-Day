Lookarounds

Check the text beside a match. Only the main pattern gets highlighted.

Positive lookahead
\d+(?=kg)

In “12kg”, match 12 when kg follows.

Negative lookahead
cat(?!fish)

In “cat catfish”, match the first cat because fish does not follow.

Positive lookbehind
(?<=£)\d+

In “£25”, match 25 when £ comes before it.

Negative lookbehind
(?<!X)R\d+

In “R2 XR7”, match R2 because X does not come before its R.
